What I need from you

  • Which funder or bank, and how much you are applying for.
  • Company registration details (or your ID if not yet registered — I will say if the funder requires registration first).
  • Your numbers: prices, costs, existing sales if trading, and what the funding buys.
  • CVs or short backgrounds of the owner and any key people.
  • Quotes for the equipment or stock the funding buys, if you have them.

I write from what you send. I do not invent revenue, customers, qualifications or track record — a plan built on invented facts fails at verification, and you carry that risk, not me.
