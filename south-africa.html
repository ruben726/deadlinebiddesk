How it works

  1. WhatsApp or email me the tender number and the eTenders link, or the funder and the application form.
  2. I reply the same day with the free scoring map (tenders) or funder checklist (funding applications).
  3. You approve the fixed price in writing.
  4. You send the facts: company documents, CSD registration number, BBBEE certificate or affidavit, tax compliance status, past projects, staff and their qualifications.
  5. I deliver the written response by the agreed date. You check it, sign it and submit it.

What I need from you

  • CSD registration number and company registration documents.
  • BBBEE certificate or sworn affidavit, and tax compliance status.
  • CIDB registration and grading, where the tender requires it.
  • Past projects: client, scope, value, dates, completion certificates or references if you have them.
  • Key staff, their real qualifications and their CVs.
  • Your pricing and the bill of quantities, where one applies.

I write from what you send. I do not create certificates, references, experience or numbers that do not exist, and I will not sign anything on your behalf.
