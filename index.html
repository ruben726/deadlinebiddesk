How it works

  1. You send the tender link. One email with the portal link or the tender reference. Nothing else needed at this point.
  2. I send you a market brief, free. Built from published contract award records: what this buyer has awarded before and to whom, the value range of comparable contracts, and who else wins work in the same category. No charge, no obligation.
  3. You approve in writing. One email saying go ahead on a named product at the stated fixed price. That email is the contract.
  4. It is delivered. Compliance Pack within 12 hours. Full Tender Response 48 hours before your deadline.
  5. You pay. The Compliance Pack is invoiced on delivery. The Full Tender Response takes a 50% deposit to start, balance on delivery.

What you have to supply

I write. I do not invent facts about your company, and a bid that invents facts loses on verification. Before drafting starts, in writing, I need:

  • The tender documents themselves — the ITT, specification and response templates you download from the buyer's portal. I cannot access buyer portals; you are the registered bidder.
  • Two or three relevant case studies or past contracts — client type, scope, value, dates, outcome.
  • Named staff for the roles the tender asks about, with their real qualifications and experience.
  • Accreditations, certifications and registrations you actually hold, with numbers and expiry dates.
  • Your pricing, or the pricing decisions already made — I do not set your price.
  • Any policies the tender calls for, or a clear statement that you do not yet have them.

Send it as documents, notes, or plain bullet points in an email. It does not have to be tidy. It does have to be true, and it has to be in writing.

What I do not do

  • No calls, no video meetings, no site visits. Everything is in writing, which also means every instruction is on the record.

  • No pricing strategy, legal advice or accounting advice.
  • No submission on your behalf. You hold the portal account and press submit.
